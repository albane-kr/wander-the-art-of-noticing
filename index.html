No space to breathe

A companion that meets you in motion

Wander is a handheld, screen‑free companion. Quiet by design, it draws your attention back to what surrounds you — the texture beneath your fingers, the shift of light, the sounds that sit beneath the city.

You don't need an app or a subscription. Only a small weight in your palm, and a walk that becomes more present with each step.

Each session is shaped by your surroundings. Wander suggests observations that make sense for your unique experience — the presence of trees, the rhythm of a street. It never asks you to notice waves when there are none.

What we believe

  • Tactile first

    Texture is the starting point. The object invites slow attention and rewards noticing.

  • Quiet by design

    No screen. No alerts. The absence of noise is intentional, it creates space for your senses to return.

  • Grounded & honest

    No wellness theatre. No pseudo‑science. Just a simple tool, shaped by real user experience.

  • Accessible

    No special posture. No special place. Just a walk you were already taking.

  • Patience

    The first sessions may feel unfamiliar. With time, the practice settles in naturally.

  • Presence over Performance

    There is nothing to track or optimize. The value comes from noticing, not measuring.

About this project

Wander was created by Albane, Nathalie, May, Sam, Zino, and Ujjwal — a student team from the Master in Computer Science: Innovation for Change in a Digital Society at Malmö University, Sweden. The project grew from shared research into how nature supports mental wellbeing, and from a frustration with digital wellness tools that add to the noise they claim to quiet.

Through user testing and physical prototyping, the idea took shape around a simple question: what if a tool could gently guide attention toward natural elements during intentional walks in urban environments?
