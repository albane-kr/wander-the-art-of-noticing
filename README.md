## About the project

Wander was created by **Albane**, **Nathalie**, **May**, **Zino**, **Sam**, and **Ujjwal** — a student team from the Master in Computer Science: Innovation for Change in a Digital Society at Malmö University, Sweden. It grew from shared research into the effects of nature on mental wellbeing, and a frustration with digital wellness tools that add to the noise they promise to quiet.

The device is a pebble-like object — screen-free, quiet — that guides a user's attention during 15–20 minute intentional walks through gentle sensory prompts. No app. No subscription. Just texture, motion, and attention.
